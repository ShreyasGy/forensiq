CIRCUMSTANCES OF DEATH
================================================================================
The decedent was discovered at approximately 06:42 AM on March 15, 2024, in
the wooded area adjacent to Millfield Park, Forensiq County. She was found
face-down in approximately 6 inches of standing rainwater near the north
perimeter trail. Discovery was made by a jogger, Marcus Webb (DOB 04-Sep-1987),
who called 911 immediately. Police arrived at 06:55 AM.

Scene observations documented by Det. Cross include: significant flattened
vegetation consistent with a struggle, broken tree branches at mid-height,
and drag marks in the mud extending approximately 12 metres from the nearest
paved footpath. No identification was recovered from the body or surrounding
area. The decedent was barefoot; no footwear was located at the scene. Missing
persons queries are ongoing.

================================================================================
MEDICAL EXAMINER'S OPINION
================================================================================
This case presents a multi-mechanism homicide of high forensic complexity.
The evidence supports the following probable sequence of events:

(1) INCAPACITATION: The decedent was administered GHB (28 mg/L) and elevated
    diazepam (0.4 mg/L), likely without consent, sufficient to impair or
    eliminate her ability to resist.

(2) BLUNT FORCE ASSAULT: A single, forceful blow to the right posterior skull
    caused the observed occipital fracture and acute subdural haematoma.

(3) STRANGULATION: Horizontal ligature strangulation was applied, causing
    hyoid fracture, petechial haemorrhage, and asphyxiation as the ultimate
    cause of death.

The defensive incised wounds on the hands and forearms confirm the victim was
conscious and resistant during at least part of the assault. The ligature mark
orientation rules out hanging. Fixed lividity inconsistent with the recovery
position CONFIRMS the body was moved to Millfield Park after death.

The actual primary scene of death has not been identified.
The unknown pregnancy (11–12 weeks) may be forensically significant and
warrants DNA profiling of fetal tissue to establish paternity.
